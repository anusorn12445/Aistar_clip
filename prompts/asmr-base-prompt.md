# 🎧 ASMR — Base Prompt (ก้อนแม่หมวด ASMR)

```
A 4-second vertical 9:16 ASMR product video WITH FULL AUDIO — close-mic sound design and a calm Thai female whisper — filmed like a real customer clip, one continuous macro take.

Scene: extreme macro on [PRODUCT], resting on [SURFACE] in soft warm light. Exactly two hands, both hers, slowly [ACTION — เช่น แกะ/บิดฝา/กด/สัมผัสพื้นผิว] with deliberate, satisfying motion.

Product ground truth from the reference photo: copy [PRODUCT] exactly — identical shape, proportions, colors, cap, logo and Thai label text. Matte stays matte, glossy stays glossy. Never redesign, relabel, restyle or swap it for a lookalike.

Camera: one continuous macro shot, shallow depth of field, soft warm light, no cuts (long take).
Audio: close-mic ASMR — [เสียงเด่น เช่น seal-peel crackle / cap twist click / fingertip friction], near-silent room tone, no music. In the last ~3 seconds VOICE ([โทนเสียง เช่น calm Thai female whisper]) says exactly, at a natural pace: "[บทพูด ~7-10 พยางค์]". She speaks only this exact line, no improvisation.

AVOID: product looking different from the reference image, product redesigned or relabeled, generic lookalike product replacing the real one, label text turning to gibberish, logo changing style or position, product colors shifted by the lighting, texture or material changing from the reference, more than two hands visible at any moment, a third hand appearing during motion, someone else's hands reaching in, disembodied hands or arms, hard cuts or camera jumps mid-clip, scene switching, morphing or warping between frames, flicker or ghosting trails, lip-sync drifting from the audio, silent clip with no audio, background music, watermark or burned-in subtitles.
```

---

**ช่องแทนที่:** `[PRODUCT]` `[SURFACE]` `[ACTION]` `[เสียงเด่น]` `[โทนเสียง]` `[บทพูด]`

**ล็อกที่ฝังมาแล้ว:** WITH FULL AUDIO (กันคลิปเงียบ) · close-mic + no music (ASMR) · exactly two hands (ล็อกมือ) · product ground truth (ล็อกสินค้าตามรูป) · long take (ไม่ตัดฉาก) · speaks only this line (กันพูดมั่ว) · AVOID เรียงลำดับ สินค้า→มือ→ฉาก→morph→เสียง→watermark

**ก่อนกด:** แนบรูปสินค้าจริงเป็น reference · บท ~7-10 พยางค์ (พูดจบใน ~3 วิของคลิป 4 วิ) · เจนภาพนิ่งผ่านก่อนค่อยเจนวิดีโอ
